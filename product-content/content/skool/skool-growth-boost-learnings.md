## 2. What the founders said (the rules, in priority order)

### How Growth Boost decides who to push

Skool computes an **efficiency score** per group: *"a group's ability to convert visitors into customers and retain them over time."* Inputs:

1. Conversion rate (about page → paid)
2. Price
3. Retention
4. Growth Boost percentage (your slider)

From these they compute **how fast they get their ad money back**. Faster payback → more spend. *"The goal of Skool is to spend as much money promoting your group as possible, but Skool is constrained by these things."* They spend more than they take.

### The four stages

| Stage | Condition | What Skool does |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Not enough data | < 100 about-page visitors **or** < 1 paid signup in the last 30 days, **from a specific traffic source** | Carousel trickle (one ad = your cover image) to help you get to a score |
| 2. Enough data, stats not good enough | e.g. payback too long | Same carousel trickle, re-tested periodically to see if stats improved |
| 3. Good stats | payback acceptable | Dedicated campaign, 20+ creatives, **$1–5k/day** on your group |
| 4. Great stats | phenomenal | Partnership campaign, **$5–10k+/day**, they clip your social content into custom video ads |

*"In these first two stages you really do need to push it yourself. You need to be making content, putting your Skool link in your bio, pushing traffic to your about page, looking after your community and generating some stats."*

### The tips, verbatim intent

1. **Skool link in bio on all platforms** (Instagram, YouTube, YouTube descriptions, everything). Skool needs to *see where traffic comes from* — attribution is part of the score. Push reels/stories/videos at the about page.
2. **Packaging.** The cover image, title, description on Discovery, plus the about-page video, photos and description. *"We run your cover image as an ad"* — the cover image **is** the ad. Test: *"if a stranger saw this, would they understand what the group is about?"* Benchmark against what's trending in your **category** on Discovery (every category has its own trending list, not just the main one).
3. **Add an annual option, 30–50% off.** Under 20% does nothing. Even if only 10–20% take it, Skool collects months of revenue up front, recoups faster, and pushes harder. *"Even at a 50% discount you collect six months in month one."* Top groups shown: 67%, 49%, 30% off.
4. **If Growth Boost isn't doing anything, drag the slider up high (70%+), then dial it back once it works.** 30 → 70 *"more than doubles the efficiency"*. Groups that were never advertised moved straight into the efficient category *"like tomorrow"*. *"Even if you just make it work at 70, at least that's better than not working at 30."* Once it's working you don't need to change anything. Bid more, always get more.
5. **Cheap groups have thin margins.** *"If a group is cheap, like 10 bucks or less, the stats have to be pretty good for it to work. The lower the price, the less forgiving the margins."*
6. **Check your free-trial conversion rate.** Platform average ≈ 40%; they've seen 80% and 8%. Below ~30%, consider turning the trial off. **The reset added per-group trial-conversion scoring** — groups with bad trial conversion that were getting boosted before *may now not be*.
7. **Doubling about-page conversion or halving churn takes months. Moving the slider takes one click.** That's why they say slider first.
8. **Focus on making the group good.** Every metric moves together.
9. **Don't reprice based on what Discovery shows today.** It used to over-index on $9 groups (people repriced to $9 for that reason — *"oh shit"*), now it leans a bit expensive, they're balancing it. It will keep changing.
10. **Ad fatigue is real** — if a boost was working and stopped, the creative may need replenishing.
11. **Reviews now show in Google search results** above your YouTube channel. Get reviews.
12. **Discovery now auto-translates UI + content per language** — a separate Discovery per language. Currencies and local payment methods coming.

### Why the reset matters for reading our numbers

They stopped all campaigns, rebuilt them across different ad accounts, and relaunched. *"It will take one to two weeks to get back to the same scale of spend."* *"If Growth Boost was working before and now it's working less well, it doesn't mean you necessarily need to change a bunch of stuff."*

## 7. What not to do

- **Do not push the slider past 70.** At $9 you already keep $2.70 a seat; the slider is not the constraint — AVC was cut four days after it went to 70.
- **Do not wait for member 110 or spot 14.** The countdown is holding both rooms at the one price Growth Boost cannot work at.
- **Do not run CCM's trial another month.**
- **Do not send cold traffic to CCM's about page until it converts strangers.** The page is the leak, not the traffic.
- **Do not let the two rooms look like one template.** One cover, one description shape, one course shape across two rooms is what got struck in June.
- **Do not delete the 350 in a hurry.** Message first, decide with numbers.
- **Do not reprice day by day off Discovery** — the founders' warning stands. $29 and $49 are the prices you already announced, not a reaction.
